This website provides an interpretable prognosis estimate for neonates with necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC). It is designed to support prognostic review after NEC has been identified; it is not a tool for diagnosing whether a patient has NEC.

What the page does

A CatBoost model combines 29 variables—3 clinical features, 9 laboratory features, and 17 structured abdominal X-ray signs—to estimate the probability that the model prediction favors an adverse prognosis.

How to use the page

  1. Enter the three required clinical values and all available laboratory results.
  2. Select only X-ray signs that are clearly present.
  3. Click Predict to view the predicted prognosis and adverse-prognosis probability.
  4. Review the feature-level SHAP explanation together with the patient's full clinical context.

Understanding the SHAP explanation

  • Red / positive SHAP: the feature shifts this individual prediction toward an adverse prognosis.
  • Blue / negative SHAP: the feature shifts this individual prediction toward a benign prognosis.
  • SHAP describes how the model used a feature for this prediction. It does not establish a causal or biological effect.

Important input notes

  • Do not enter patient names, hospitalization numbers, or other identifiers; they are not model inputs.
  • The current X-ray controls are binary: selected means clearly present (1), and unselected means clearly absent (−1).
  • If an X-ray sign is uncertain or not reported, do not assume that an unselected box correctly represents the clinical record.

Scope and limitations

The model was developed from a single-center cohort and requires further external and prospective validation. The probability is a model estimate, not a definitive clinical outcome. Interpret it alongside imaging, laboratory trends, treatment response, comorbidities, and professional clinical judgment.

For clinical reference and research use only. This website does not replace diagnosis, treatment decisions, or assessment by qualified clinicians.
